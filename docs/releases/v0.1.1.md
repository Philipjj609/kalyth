# Kalyth 0.1.1, the hosted demo's allowance

Tagged on 30 October 2026, from [0.1.0](v0.1.0.md), the competition release. It changes only the
hosted demo's live-run allowance and the dates it follows; the product is unchanged.

- **The official Judging Period** runs from 9 December 2026 at 09:00 PT to 23 December at 12:00 PT
  (the hackathon rules). 0.1.0 assumed 1 to 15 December. The public's larger allowance now follows
  the official period, and live runs end when it ends. The demo stays up until then.
- **Judges share 0.30 USD a day**, from now until the Judging Period ends, within a hard total of
  8.00 USD over everything the judges' pool has ever spent. The public keeps its pool, 0.07 USD a
  day and 0.15 USD during judging, within its 4.49 USD total. Each copy keeps its three live runs a
  day, and the per-address limits are unchanged.
- **Today's caps are set again** when the new schedule starts, so the change applies at once.

Everything else, including the recorded runs and the tests, is as in 0.1.0.
